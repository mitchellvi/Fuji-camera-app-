Third-party film profile assets used by Fuji Pocket

1) Kodak Gold 200, Kodak Portra 400, Kodak Ektar 100 and Fujifilm Pro 400H LUT source data
   Source: ComfyUI-Darkroom by Jeremie Louvaert, https://github.com/jeremieLouvaert/ComfyUI-Darkroom
   The bundled spectral LUTs are derived from the MIT-licensed spectral_film_lut project by Jan Lohse.
   License: MIT. The original source LUTs model scene-linear sRGB input and sRGB output.
   Fuji Pocket's bundled copies are resampled for display-referred sRGB camera input.
   Source LUT filenames:
   - gold_200_endura_premier.cube
   - portra_400_endura_premier.cube
   - ektar_100_endura_premier.cube
   - fuji_pro_400h_ca_maxima.cube

2) Fujifilm Classic Chrome preset
   Source: EmulsionPresets by peva3, https://github.com/peva3/EmulsionPresets
   File: Fujifilm Classic Chrome.xmp
   License: MIT. This is a Lightroom-style creative preset, not a manufacturer-issued LUT.
   Classic Chrome itself is Fujifilm's digital film simulation, not an analogue film stock.
